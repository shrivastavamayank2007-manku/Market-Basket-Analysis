How to interpret the metrics

Support

The percentage of all transactions containing the itemset. Higher support means the pattern appears more often.

Confidence

Among transactions containing the left-hand side, the percentage that also contain the right-hand side.

Lift

Confidence divided by the support of the right-hand side. Lift > 1 suggests a positive association; around 1 suggests little association.

Apriori principle

If an itemset is frequent, all its non-empty subsets must also be frequent. If a subset is infrequent, its supersets can be pruned.
